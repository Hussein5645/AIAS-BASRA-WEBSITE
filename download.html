OFFICIAL MOBILE WEB APP AIAS Basra Community Installable on Android & iOS

Install the Community app on your Android device. It will appear on your home screen and app drawer just like a native app.

How to install on Android
  1. Tap the “Install on Android” button above (or tap your browser menu ⋮).
  2. Select “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
  3. Tap “Install” to confirm and launch from your home screen.

Add the Community app to your iPhone or iPad home screen via Safari for a full-screen, app-like experience.

How to install on iPhone & iPad (Safari)
  1. Open this website in Safari and tap the Share button ⎋.
  2. Scroll down the share sheet and tap “Add to Home Screen” ⊞.
  3. Tap “Add” in the top right corner. The icon will appear on your home screen.
